HOW TO USE YOUR SIGIL

  1. Look at it until the letters disappear. Let it stop being words and become a pure shape to you.
  2. Charge it. Enter a still, focused state through slow breathing, meditation, drumming, dance, or whatever empties your mind. At the peak, gaze at the sigil and pour your will into it.
  3. Release it. Burn it, bury it, or put it away, and let the intention go from your conscious mind.
  4. Forget it. Chaos magicians hold that a sigil works best when you stop thinking about it, so your deeper mind can carry it out.
